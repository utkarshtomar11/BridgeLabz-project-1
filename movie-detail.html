About the Movie

In 1942, during World War II, J. Robert Oppenheimer, a theoretical physicist, is recruited by the United States Army to work on the Manhattan Project, a top-secret program to develop the first nuclear weapons.

As the scientific director of the Los Alamos Laboratory in New Mexico, Oppenheimer leads a team of brilliant scientists in an intense race against time. The project faces numerous technical challenges and moral dilemmas as it progresses toward its ultimate goal.

The film chronicles the complex life of Oppenheimer, exploring his scientific genius, political entanglements, and the profound ethical questions that arise from the creation of weapons of mass destruction. After the war, Oppenheimer becomes a controversial figure as he grapples with the implications of his work.
